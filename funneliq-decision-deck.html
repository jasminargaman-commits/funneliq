Two Decisions the Data Changed

Reconsider the follow-up cutoff

Lowest dropout: between rounds 3 and 4.

Observed dropout rate by transition

Investigate the jump entering round 5 (29.2%) before changing the sequence. Dropout between rounds 3 and 4 is only 10.4%.

All recorded closes follow round 5. This dataset cannot establish the effect of stopping earlier.

Same ₪50K. Different allocations.

Compare predicted profit for the same spend.

Model-simulated profit · not realized profit

25 campaigns × ₪2,000 produced the highest simulated profit among the tested allocations. Validate with a pilot.

06 / 09
What the Analyses Have in Common

Two patterns recur across the analyses

Fewer calls to close

Associated with

  • Longer customer lifetime
  • Higher upsell likelihood
  • Higher profit

Mid-budget campaigns

₪2,000–5,000 per campaign

Associated with

  • Higher conversion rates
  • Higher predicted referral likelihood

The patterns recur across models and analyses of the same dataset. They still need validation on new data.

These are observational associations, not evidence of causality.
